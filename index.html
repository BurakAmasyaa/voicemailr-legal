Frequently asked questions

How does VoiceMailr transcribe my voice?

VoiceMailr uses Apple's built-in Speech framework to convert your voice into text. Transcription happens through Apple's system services on your device.

Where does my note go after I record it?

Your note is prepared as an email and sent to the recipient you've configured in Settings. You control who receives your notes.

Can I change the recipient or language?

Yes. Open Settings from the main screen to update your recipient addresses, transcription language, and default note type at any time.

What's the difference between Quick and Controlled mode?

Quick mode sends your note automatically as soon as recording stops. Controlled mode shows you the transcript first, so you can review or edit before sending.

Is my data shared with third parties?

See our Privacy Policy for full details on how your data is handled.

How do I delete my data?

Notes and preferences are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes this data. Contact us if you need further assistance.
